Privacy

This is a one-person consulting business and the site collects very little. Here is all of it.

When you use the contact form

The form sends your name, email, company and message to my inbox through a service called Formspree. I use it to reply to you. You are not added to a mailing list, and it is not sold or shared.

When you just visit

I use a tool from Apollo that estimates which company a visitor comes from, based on the network address your browser connects with. It tells me that somebody at a given company read a page. It does not tell me who you are, and I make no attempt to find out. It may set a cookie so that a return visit is recognised as the same browser.

There is no advertising here, no advertising trackers, and nothing is sold to anyone.

Questions, or want anything removed

Email me at mike@seacloudconsulting.com and I will delete whatever I hold.
